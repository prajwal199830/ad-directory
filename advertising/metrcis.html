ADVERTISING FUNDAMENTALS

How Advertisers Measure Campaigns

Advertising campaigns can be priced and evaluated using different metrics depending on the campaign objective.

Advertisers may pay based on impressions, clicks, actions, leads, installs or a fixed advertising period. Publishers and advertisers also use performance metrics to understand how campaigns are performing.

Understanding these terms helps explain how money moves through the digital advertising ecosystem and how campaign performance is measured.

UNDERSTANDING VIEWABILITY

Served Does Not Always Mean Viewable

An impression can be served without the advertisement actually being visible to the user.

What is Viewability?

Viewability measures whether an advertisement had an opportunity to be seen by the user.

For example, an advertisement may be loaded below the visible portion of a webpage. The ad can be served even though the user has not yet scrolled far enough to see it.

Common industry viewability definitions use both the percentage of the advertisement that is visible and the amount of time it remains visible.

Display Advertising A commonly used MRC/IAB measurement threshold is at least 50% of the ad's pixels in view for at least 1 continuous second.
Video Advertising A commonly used MRC/IAB measurement threshold is at least 50% of the video's pixels in view for at least 2 continuous seconds.
