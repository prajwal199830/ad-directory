What is Open Bidding?

Open Bidding is a server-side auction approach integrated with Google Ad Manager that allows participating demand partners to compete for eligible publisher inventory.

Open Bidding

Open Bidding is a programmatic monetization solution available within Google Ad Manager. It allows publishers to invite eligible third-party demand partners to compete in an auction for their advertising inventory.

Instead of relying on a single demand source, participating demand partners can submit bids for an impression. Google Ad Manager can then use the auction outcome as part of its ad-serving decision.

Open Bidding is different from client-side header bidding because the demand auction is handled through server-side infrastructure rather than requiring every demand partner to make a direct browser request.

Why Do Publishers Use Open Bidding?

Open Bidding can give publishers another way to bring demand sources into competition for eligible advertising inventory.

More Demand Competition

Publishers can work with multiple participating demand partners instead of depending entirely on a single source of demand.

The Main Idea

Eligible demand partners can submit bids for available inventory. The resulting competition can create additional opportunities for publishers to improve yield and monetization.

Open Bidding vs Header Bidding

Both approaches allow multiple demand sources to compete, but their auction architecture and implementation can differ.

Feature Open Bidding Header Bidding
Auction environment Server-side infrastructure Client-side or server-side
Ad server integration Closely integrated with Google Ad Manager Bid results are typically passed to the ad server
Browser involvement Lower direct demand-request involvement Can be higher for client-side implementations
Demand competition Participating demand partners Configured demand partners
Main consideration Demand availability and auction performance Latency, configuration and demand coverage
Publisher setup Configured through supported Google Ad Manager functionality Usually requires a header bidding solution or wrapper

Key Open Bidding Terms

Open Bidding A server-side auction approach available through Google Ad Manager.
Google Ad Manager Google's ad management platform used by publishers to manage advertising inventory.
Demand Partner A participating demand source that can submit bids for eligible inventory.
Bid The value submitted by a demand source for an advertising opportunity.
Auction The process through which eligible demand competes for inventory.
Yield The value generated from available advertising inventory.
Floor Price A minimum value that may be applied to eligible inventory.
Ad Inventory Advertising opportunities available for buyers and demand sources.
