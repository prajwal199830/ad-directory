What is Header Bidding?

Advertising Guide • Programmatic Advertising • AdTech

Header bidding is a programmatic advertising technique that allows publishers to offer an ad impression to multiple demand partners simultaneously before the final ad-serving decision is made.

By increasing competition among eligible demand partners, header bidding can help publishers improve the value of their ad inventory and potentially increase advertising revenue.

Publisher Inventory → Multiple Demand Partners → Competing Bids → Ad Server → Ad Impression

Why is Header Bidding Used?

Header bidding is primarily used to increase competition among multiple demand sources for the same ad impression.

Instead of relying on a single demand source, publishers can allow multiple demand partners to compete for an impression. The resulting bids can then compete with other eligible demand, helping the publisher identify the most valuable opportunity.

In simple terms

More eligible demand competing for an impression → greater auction competition → potentially higher publisher revenue.

Types of Header Bidding

Header bidding is commonly implemented using two broad approaches: client-side and server-side.

1. Client-Side Header Bidding

In client-side header bidding, the auction takes place in the user's browser or app environment before the ad request is sent to the ad server.

Advantages

  • Direct interaction with demand partners
  • Greater visibility into bid responses
  • Can provide direct access to multiple demand partners

Challenges

  • Browser latency
  • Additional browser-side requests
  • Page performance considerations
  • Limits on how many partners can efficiently participate

2. Server-Side Header Bidding

In server-side header bidding, the auction is handled through a server or intermediary rather than requiring each demand partner to communicate directly with the user's browser.

Advantages

  • Can support a larger number of demand partners
  • Reduces browser-side requests
  • Can reduce the workload on the user's browser
  • Potentially improves page performance

Challenges

  • Potentially less transparency
  • Additional server-side infrastructure
  • Some demand partners may have less access to certain user signals

Client-Side vs Server-Side Header Bidding

Feature Client-Side Server-Side
Auction location Browser / app environment Server / intermediary
Browser requests Higher Lower
Number of partners Can be limited Can scale more easily
Transparency Generally higher Can be lower
Page performance Can be affected by additional requests Can reduce browser workload

Why is Header Bidding Important for Publishers?

Advertising inventory is a valuable resource for publishers. Header bidding gives publishers a way to expose eligible impressions to multiple sources of demand and increase competition.

However, higher competition does not automatically guarantee higher revenue. Results depend on factors such as demand quality, bid participation, latency, floor prices, user signals, inventory quality and the publisher's overall monetization strategy.
