What is Digital Advertising?

Digital advertising is the use of online platforms, websites, applications, connected television and other digital channels to deliver advertising to audiences. Modern digital advertising includes direct campaigns, programmatic advertising, search advertising, display advertising, video advertising and connected TV.

What is AdTech?

AdTech, short for advertising technology, refers to the technologies and platforms used to buy, sell, deliver, measure and optimize digital advertising. The ecosystem includes publishers, advertisers, agencies, DSPs, SSPs, ad exchanges, ad servers, data platforms and measurement technologies.

Programmatic Advertising

Programmatic advertising uses automated technology to buy and sell digital advertising inventory. Common components include demand-side platforms, supply-side platforms, ad exchanges, real-time bidding and header bidding. Publishers use these technologies to connect their inventory with advertising demand.

Publisher Monetization

Publisher advertising revenue can depend on traffic, impressions, CPM, eCPM, fill rate, viewability, demand competition, ad formats and audience characteristics. Understanding these metrics can help publishers evaluate advertising performance and monetization opportunities.

CTV and Video Advertising

Connected TV and digital video advertising are growing areas of the digital advertising ecosystem. Technologies such as VAST, video ad servers, client-side ad insertion and server-side ad insertion help deliver advertising across websites, applications, streaming services and connected television platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is digital advertising?

Digital advertising is advertising delivered through digital channels such as websites, mobile applications, search engines, online video and connected television.

What is AdTech?

AdTech is the technology ecosystem used to buy, sell, deliver, measure and optimize digital advertising.

What is programmatic advertising?

Programmatic advertising uses automated technology to buy and sell digital advertising inventory, often using platforms such as DSPs, SSPs and ad exchanges.

What is Google Ad Manager?

Google Ad Manager is an advertising platform used by publishers to manage digital advertising inventory, campaigns, demand and monetization.

What is header bidding?

Header bidding is an auction technology that allows publishers to offer advertising inventory to multiple demand sources before selecting a winning ad opportunity.
