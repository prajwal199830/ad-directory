Digital Advertising and AdTech

PubsRate provides advertising news, AdTech resources and practical guides covering the digital advertising ecosystem. The advertising section is designed for publishers, advertisers, AdOps professionals, developers and other people working in digital media.

Programmatic Advertising

Programmatic advertising uses technology and automated systems to buy and sell digital advertising inventory. PubsRate covers topics including DSPs, SSPs, ad exchanges, header bidding, real-time bidding and publisher monetization.

Google Ad Manager and Publisher Advertising

Google Ad Manager is widely used by publishers to manage digital advertising inventory, campaigns, demand sources and monetization. PubsRate provides guides and updates related to Google Ad Manager and other publisher advertising technologies.

CTV, Video and Digital Advertising

Connected TV and digital video advertising are important parts of the modern advertising ecosystem. PubsRate covers CTV, OTT, VAST, video advertising and related AdTech technologies used by publishers and advertisers.

AI and Advertising

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being used across advertising, including audience analysis, campaign optimization, creative development and publisher workflows. PubsRate follows important developments in AI and digital advertising.

Publisher Monetization

Publisher monetization depends on factors such as advertising demand, traffic quality, viewability, ad formats, CPM, eCPM, fill rate and programmatic competition. Our resources help publishers understand the technologies and concepts behind digital advertising revenue.
