VIDEO ADVERTISING

How Video Ads Reach the Viewer

Digital video advertising can use different technologies depending on where the ad is requested, selected and inserted.

Video advertising can be delivered through different ad insertion approaches. The technology used can affect where the ad is inserted and how the player receives the advertisement.

Three important concepts are SSAI, CSAI and DAI. Wrappers and macros are additional components that can be encountered while working with video advertising tags and requests.

CORE CONCEPTS

Five Video Advertising Concepts

Start with these five concepts to understand the fundamentals of digital video ad insertion.

01
🖥️

SSAI

Server-Side Ad Insertion

Server-Side Ad Insertion is an ad insertion technique where advertisements are inserted into the video stream on the server side before the content reaches the user's device.

SERVER SIDE
02
▶️

CSAI

Client-Side Ad Insertion

Client-Side Ad Insertion is an ad insertion technique where the video player on the user's device requests, fetches and loads the advertisement separately from the main content.

CLIENT SIDE
03
🎯

DAI

Dynamic Ad Insertion

Dynamic Ad Insertion is a technique where advertisements can be selected and inserted dynamically using available information such as location, device, content or other targeting signals.

DYNAMIC
04
W

Wrapper

Ad Tag Container

A wrapper is an advertising tag that references another ad tag. It can be used to pass additional information and support functions such as tracking, measurement or additional ad requests.

AD TAG
05
%

Macros

Dynamic Placeholders

Macros are placeholders used in advertising tags or URLs. They can be replaced with actual values when the request or tracking event is processed.

SSAI vs CSAI vs DAI

A simple comparison of the three main concepts covered on this page.
