What Are Programmatic Standards?

Programmatic standards are specifications and industry frameworks designed to improve interoperability, transparency, trust and efficiency across digital advertising.

Standards & Transparency

Programmatic advertising involves publishers, advertisers, agencies, DSPs, SSPs, ad exchanges, ad servers and other technology providers. Standards help these participants communicate and exchange information consistently.

Transparency frameworks can help participants understand who is authorized to sell advertising inventory, which entities are involved in the supply chain, and how an impression moves through the programmatic ecosystem.

Common technologies and standards include ads.txt, app-ads.txt, sellers.json, the SupplyChain object and OpenRTB specifications. Each addresses a different part of the advertising ecosystem.

Why Does Programmatic Transparency Matter?

Transparency helps advertising participants better understand inventory ownership, authorized sellers and the programmatic supply chain.

Authorized Selling

ads.txt and app-ads.txt allow publishers and app developers to publicly identify advertising systems that are authorized to sell their inventory.

The Main Idea

A transparent programmatic ecosystem makes it easier for buyers and sellers to understand the entities involved in an advertising transaction and evaluate the quality and legitimacy of supply.

Key Programmatic Standards & Transparency Terms

ads.txt A standard that allows publishers to publicly identify authorized sellers of their digital advertising inventory.
app-ads.txt An extension of ads.txt designed for mobile and connected television application inventory.
sellers.json A transparency mechanism that provides information about entities involved in selling advertising inventory.
SupplyChain A mechanism used to describe the entities involved in the programmatic advertising supply chain.
OpenRTB An industry specification used to facilitate communication between buyers and sellers in real-time bidding.
Authorized Seller An advertising system that a publisher has authorized to sell its inventory.
Reseller An entity authorized to resell advertising inventory on behalf of another seller or publisher.
Supply Chain The sequence of publishers, sellers, exchanges, SSPs and other entities involved in a programmatic transaction.
