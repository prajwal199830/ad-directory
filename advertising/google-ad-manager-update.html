Google Introduces Ask Ad Manager (Beta), an AI-Powered Assistant That Helps Publishers Generate Custom Performance Reports, Investigate Ad Delivery Issues, and Quickly Navigate to Relevant Ad Manager Settings

Published: August 14, 2026   •   Category: Google Ad Manager
🤖

AI-Powered Assistance for Google Ad Manager

Google has introduced Ask Ad Manager (Beta), a conversational AI assistant designed to help publishers work with Google Ad Manager more efficiently.

What is Ask Ad Manager?

Ask Ad Manager is a conversational AI experience within Google Ad Manager that allows publishers to use natural-language queries to access information, investigate issues and navigate relevant platform settings.

Instead of manually searching through multiple areas of the platform, publishers can describe what they are trying to accomplish and use the assistant to find relevant information.

What Can Publishers Do With Ask Ad Manager?

Generate Custom Performance Reports

Publishers can use conversational queries to request performance information and generate customized reports.

Investigate Ad Delivery Issues

Publishers can describe potential delivery problems using natural language and use the assistant to investigate relevant information.

Navigate to Relevant Settings

Ask Ad Manager can help publishers find relevant settings within Google Ad Manager without manually searching through multiple sections of the platform.

Why This Matters for Publishers

Google Ad Manager contains a wide range of reports, configurations and troubleshooting tools. Finding the right information can sometimes require navigating through multiple areas of an ad server.

A conversational interface could simplify these workflows by allowing publishers to describe what they need using natural language.

Key Takeaways

  • Ask Ad Manager is currently available in beta.
  • Publishers can interact with the assistant using natural-language queries.
  • The assistant can help with performance reporting and analysis.
  • Publishers can use it to investigate potential ad delivery issues.
  • The tool can help users locate relevant Ad Manager settings.

What This Could Mean for AdTech

The introduction of conversational AI into publisher platforms represents a broader shift toward making complex advertising technology easier to operate.

As AI assistants become more integrated into advertising platforms, publishers may increasingly use natural-language interfaces for reporting, troubleshooting and optimization workflows.
