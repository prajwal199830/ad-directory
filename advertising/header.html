What is Header Bidding?

Header Bidding

Header bidding is a programmatic advertising technique used by publishers to offer their ad inventory to multiple demand partners simultaneously before making a final ad decision.

It is an approach used by publishers to maximize revenue by increasing competition for their available inventory.

Why is Header Bidding Needed?

Header bidding changes how demand sources can compete for publisher inventory.

Traditional Approach

Instead of selecting an ad or line item directly from the ad server, publishers can use header bidding to give multiple demand partners an opportunity to bid for the same ad inventory.

The Main Idea

Multiple demand partners can compete for the available inventory. Increased competition can help publishers increase revenue and improve yield.

Increasing Competition

Header bidding creates an opportunity for multiple demand sources to compete for an impression.

Types of Header Bidding

Header bidding can generally be implemented through client-side or server-side approaches.

01

Client-Side Header Bidding

In client-side header bidding, the header bidding wrapper running on the webpage or app makes requests to multiple demand partners.

The bids are collected, and the auction takes place on the user's device or within the app. Once the winning bid is selected, the bid is passed to the ad server for the final ad-serving decision.

02

Server-Side Header Bidding

In server-side header bidding, the header bidding server sends requests to multiple demand partners.

The server conducts the auction, determines the winning bid, and then passes the result to the ad server for the final ad-serving decision.

Client-Side vs Server-Side Header Bidding

Both approaches enable multiple demand partners to compete, but the auction architecture is different.
