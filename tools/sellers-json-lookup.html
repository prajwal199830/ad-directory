What Is sellers.json?

sellers.json is a standardized file used in the digital advertising ecosystem to provide information about entities that participate in the selling of advertising inventory.

What Can You Find With This Tool?

The PubsRate Sellers.json Lookup lets you search seller records using seller IDs, names and domains. You can also filter results by seller type.

Seller Types

Sellers.json records can identify entities as publishers, intermediaries or both. This information can help AdTech professionals understand the role of an entity within the advertising supply chain.

Privacy

The lookup is performed directly in your browser. The pasted sellers.json data is not sent to a PubsRate server by this page.
