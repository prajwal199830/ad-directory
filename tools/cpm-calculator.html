What is CPM?

CPM stands for Cost Per Thousand impressions. It is a commonly used advertising metric that represents the cost or revenue associated with one thousand ad impressions.

CPM is commonly used by advertisers, publishers, agencies and advertising platforms to evaluate and compare advertising performance.

CPM Formula

CPM = (Advertising Revenue ÷ Ad Impressions) × 1,000

CPM Calculation Example

Suppose a publisher generates:

Advertising Revenue: ₹10,000
Ad Impressions: 500,000

CPM = (₹10,000 ÷ 500,000) × 1,000
CPM = ₹20

Why is CPM Important?

CPM helps publishers understand the revenue generated for every thousand impressions and allows them to compare advertising performance across different demand sources, campaigns and inventory types.

Advertisers can also use CPM to evaluate the cost of reaching audiences across different advertising channels and campaigns.
