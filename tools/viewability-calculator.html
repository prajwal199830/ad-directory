What Is Ad Viewability?

Ad viewability measures whether an advertisement had an opportunity to be seen by a user. It is an important metric for publishers, advertisers and AdOps teams when evaluating digital advertising performance.

How Is Viewability Calculated?

Viewability percentage is calculated by dividing viewable impressions by measurable impressions and multiplying the result by 100.

Viewability % = (Viewable Impressions ÷ Measurable Impressions) × 100

Why Is Viewability Important?

Higher viewability can indicate that advertisements are receiving more opportunities to be seen. Publishers and advertising teams can use viewability data to understand ad placement and inventory performance.
