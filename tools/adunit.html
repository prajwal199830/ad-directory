Ad Unit Naming Generator

Ad unit naming is an important part of digital advertising operations. A consistent naming convention helps publishers organize advertising inventory and makes it easier for AdOps teams to identify placements across websites and applications.

Why Use a Consistent Ad Unit Naming Convention?

Publishers can have hundreds or thousands of ad placements across different devices, page types, formats and sizes. A structured naming convention makes those placements easier to understand and manage.

  • Easier inventory management
  • Faster troubleshooting
  • Better reporting and analysis
  • Easier campaign trafficking
  • Consistent communication between AdOps teams
  • Easier identification of ad placements

Recommended Ad Unit Naming Structure

A useful naming structure can include the publisher, device, page type, ad format, ad size, placement position and environment.

For example:

site / device / page / format / size / position / environment

Who Can Use This Tool?

The Ad Unit Naming Generator can be useful for publishers, AdOps professionals, advertising operations teams, developers and anyone managing digital advertising inventory.
