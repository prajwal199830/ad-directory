What is a SupplyChain Object?

A SupplyChain Object, commonly called schain, provides information about the companies involved in the sale or resale of digital advertising inventory.

It helps buyers understand the path an impression takes through the programmatic advertising ecosystem.

Common schain Fields

  • ver — SupplyChain specification version.
  • complete — Indicates whether the supply chain is complete.
  • nodes — Contains the companies involved in the transaction path.
  • asi — Advertising system identifier, normally representing the domain of the advertising system.
  • sid — Seller identifier associated with the advertising system.
  • hp — Indicates whether the node is involved in payment handling.

Why is schain important?

SupplyChain information can improve transparency in the programmatic advertising ecosystem by helping buyers understand the entities involved in selling advertising inventory.

Publishers, SSPs, exchanges, DSPs and AdOps teams may encounter SupplyChain Objects when troubleshooting programmatic advertising requests.
