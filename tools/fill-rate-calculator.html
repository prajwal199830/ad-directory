What is Fill Rate?

Fill rate is an advertising metric that indicates the percentage of ad requests that result in an ad impression.

Publishers can use fill rate to understand how effectively their available advertising opportunities are being monetized.

Fill Rate Formula

Fill Rate = (Ad Impressions ÷ Ad Requests) × 100

Fill Rate Calculation Example

Suppose a publisher receives:

Ad Requests: 1,000,000
Ad Impressions: 850,000

Fill Rate = (850,000 ÷ 1,000,000) × 100
Fill Rate = 85%

Why is Fill Rate Important?

A low fill rate may indicate that some advertising opportunities are not being monetized.

Publishers may analyze demand sources, targeting, pricing, inventory availability, ad quality, user consent and other factors when investigating fill-rate performance.
