How the SSP Recommendation Works

PubsRate compares publisher requirements against SSP capabilities instead of simply listing SSPs.

01. Publisher Requirements

Enter your geography, inventory type, monthly impressions, CPM target and technical requirements.

02. SSP Matching

The tool checks inventory, integration, request type and monetization requirements against the SSP database.

03. Match Score

SSPs are ranked according to how closely their available capabilities match the publisher's requirements.
