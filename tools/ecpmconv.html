CPM & eCPM Formulas

CPM and eCPM are commonly used metrics in digital advertising to measure advertising value and revenue efficiency.

CPM = (Advertising Cost ÷ Impressions) × 1,000

eCPM = (Advertising Revenue ÷ Impressions) × 1,000

Revenue = (CPM × Impressions) ÷ 1,000

Example Calculation

Suppose a publisher generates $500 in advertising revenue from 100,000 impressions.

eCPM = ($500 ÷ 100,000) × 1,000

eCPM = $5.00

This means the publisher generated an effective revenue of $5 for every 1,000 impressions.

What is CPM?

CPM stands for Cost Per Mille, where "mille" means one thousand. CPM represents the cost associated with delivering 1,000 advertising impressions. Advertisers commonly use CPM when evaluating the cost of advertising inventory.

What is eCPM?

eCPM stands for Effective Cost Per Mille. It is commonly used to normalize advertising revenue against impressions and determine how much revenue is generated per 1,000 impressions.

CPM vs eCPM

CPM generally describes the cost of advertising inventory from the advertiser's perspective, while eCPM can be used to measure effective revenue or monetization performance from the publisher's perspective.

Why CPM and eCPM Matter to Publishers

  • Compare advertising performance across demand sources.
  • Understand revenue generated from impressions.
  • Compare different ad formats.
  • Analyze publisher monetization performance.
  • Evaluate changes in advertising demand.
