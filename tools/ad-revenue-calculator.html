How Ad Revenue Is Calculated

A simple way to estimate advertising revenue is to multiply the number of impressions by the eCPM and divide the result by 1,000.

Estimated Revenue = (Impressions ÷ 1,000) × eCPM

Ad Revenue Calculation Example

Suppose a publisher generates:

Ad Impressions: 1,000,000
eCPM: $2.50

Revenue = (1,000,000 ÷ 1,000) × $2.50
Estimated Revenue = $2,500

Important Note

This calculator provides an estimate based on impressions and eCPM. Actual publisher revenue can vary depending on demand sources, geography, device type, ad format, viewability, fill rate, auction dynamics, fees, invalid traffic and other factors.
