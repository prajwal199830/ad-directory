What Does This Tool Check?

PubsRate analyzes the VAST response and looks for common advertising components including the VAST version, ad type, impressions, media files, duration, click-through information and tracking events.

The tool is intended for development, troubleshooting and educational purposes. A successful XML response does not necessarily mean that an ad will successfully play in every player, device or advertising environment.
