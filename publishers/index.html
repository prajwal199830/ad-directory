Publisher CPM Rates

CPM, or cost per thousand impressions, is one of the common metrics used to understand digital advertising pricing. Publisher CPM rates can vary significantly depending on audience, geography, content category, device, ad format, seasonality, campaign objectives and advertising demand.

PubsRate provides directional CPM benchmarks and advertising information to help users research publisher media opportunities. These figures should be treated as market reference points rather than guaranteed campaign prices.
