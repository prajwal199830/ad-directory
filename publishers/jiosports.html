About JioHotstar

JioHotstar is a major streaming platform in India offering movies, originals, live sports, live events, anime, kids content and television programming.

According to JioStar's official information, JioHotstar has more than 450 million monthly average users and more than 300,000 hours of programming across 19 languages.
