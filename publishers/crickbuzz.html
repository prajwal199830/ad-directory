How the Cricbuzz CPM Calculator Works

The calculator estimates impressions using the standard CPM formula. CPM represents the cost for one thousand advertising impressions.

Estimated impressions = Campaign budget ÷ CPM × 1,000

For example, a ₹50,000 campaign using a ₹75 reference CPM would produce an estimated 666,667 impressions before accounting for campaign delivery, targeting, availability or other commercial conditions.
